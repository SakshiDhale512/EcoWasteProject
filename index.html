01 / WHO WE ARE

Less waste.
More life.

A sustainable future isn't a distant dream. It's built through the choices we make every day.

EcoWaste is an educational initiative designed to make responsible waste management easier to understand and put into practice. We connect people with practical ideas for reducing waste, sorting materials correctly, and caring for shared spaces.

02 / KNOW YOUR WASTE

Every kind of waste
has a better next step.

Knowing what you are throwing away is the first step toward handling it responsibly.

♧
01

Organic Waste

Food scraps, leaves, and garden waste can often become nutrient-rich compost.

♻
02

Recyclable Waste

Clean paper, cardboard, glass, and metals may be recyclable in your area.

◈
03

Plastic Waste

Reduce single-use plastics and check local rules before recycling packaging.

⌁
04

Electronic Waste

Phones, computers, chargers, and appliances need approved e-waste channels.

⚠
05

Hazardous Waste

Batteries, chemicals, and some household products require careful handling.

▤
06

General Waste

Items that cannot be reused or accepted for recycling may need residual disposal.

✳ Rules vary by municipality. When in doubt, check your local waste authority.
